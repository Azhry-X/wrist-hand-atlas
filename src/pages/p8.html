A · Exercise, Recovery, CRPS Exposure, Return to Work & JOSPT CTS CPG Outcome MeasuresEXERCISE & RTW
Exercise & RecoveryØsterås 2017
Hand OA exercise (Cochrane) (Østerås 2017 Cochrane) L: pain SMD −0.27 (−0.47 to −0.07), 5 trials 381 pts; function SMD −0.28 (−0.58 to 0.02); stiffness SMD −0.36 (−0.58 to −0.15); low quality
Rheumatoid hand — SARAH (RCT, n = 490) (Lamb 2015 Lancet) H: tailored exercises: MHQ function +4·3 (1·5–7·1) at 12 months
DRF recovery (cohort, n = 129) (MacDermid 2003 BMC Musculoskelet Disord) M: by 3 months median 'no pain at rest', worst pain 3/10; most recovery within six months; hardest tasks: carrying ten pounds, pushing up from a chair
CRPS-I — exposure in vivo (RCT, n = 46) (den Hollander 2016 Pain) M: graded exposure more effective than pain-contingent care for disability in patients with pain-related fear
Return to Work & Outcome MeasuresJOSPT 2019
Hand / wrist trauma (cohort, n = 383) (Neutel 2019 J Hand Surg Eur) M: time off work linked to job type, diagnosis, complication, blaming someone else, gender
Occupational upper-limb injury (cohort) (Bai 2022 Front Med) L: 107/163 (65.6%) back at work at 1 year
JOSPT CTS CPG — outcome measures (Erickson 2019 JOSPT CTS CPG) (Guideline): “should use the CTQ-SSS to assess symptoms and the ... CTQ-FS or the ... DASH questionnaire to assess function”; “should not use grip strength when assessing short-term (less than 3 months) change ... following CTR surgery”; “should not use lateral pinch strength as an outcome measure”

Tendon gliding E: straight hand → hook fist → full fist → tabletop → straight fist; smooth, pain-free excursion
Nerve gliding E: median / ulnar sliders before tensioners; stop if paraesthesia persists after the set
Proprioception & loading E: joint position sense → dart-thrower's motion → weight-bearing → perturbation → work / sport tasks
